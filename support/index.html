Awwab support

Awwab is a personal prayer tracker, made by Ahmed Nabil.

Questions, problems, or something you miss in the app: write to ahmednmourad.dev@gmail.com. Say which phone you use and what happened, and you will get an answer from the person who wrote the app.

Everything you record stays on your device, so no support request can reach your prayer log. The privacy policy, https://ahmednmourad.github.io/awwab/privacy/, says what does leave the device and why.
